The change

How the verdict is decided

Two signals the product already stores and the ranker currently ignores: experience band (your years against the job's range) and skill overlap (how much of what the job asks for you have).

ExperienceOverlap ≥ 50%25–49%Under 25%
In bandStrong matchWorth a stretchLikely under-qualified
Within 1 yrWorth a stretchWorth a stretchLikely under-qualified
2+ yrs belowLikely under-qualifiedLikely under-qualifiedLikely under-qualified

This is the first negative term in the ranking. Every existing signal only ever adds points, which is precisely why the list cannot currently say no — measured across the seed data, 0 of 32 jobs score zero.

Edge cases the prototype assumes

No skills on profile

Overlap is undefined, not zero. Show no verdict at all rather than a wrong one, and prompt the candidate to add skills.

Over-qualified

Treated as in-band for v1. Flagged as an open question — it may deserve its own verdict rather than being quietly folded in.

Job lists no skills

Fall back to the experience band alone, and say so, so the verdict is never more confident than its inputs.

Feedback

The one thing worth arguing about: is a negative verdict the right call? It will tell candidates not to apply to jobs they might have got.

Responses are saved with the prototype and read back by the PM agent.
